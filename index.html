Mountain — Free, Local-First & Zero-Knowledge Password Manager

A free, open-source password manager running entirely client-side in your browser. All credentials are encrypted with AES-256-GCM and protected by a 12-word BIP-39 recovery phrase. Zero cloud servers, zero tracking, zero accounts.

Core Architecture

  • Deterministic Master Identity: Standardized 12-word BIP-39 mnemonic phrase acts as the root master key.
  • Client-Side Authenticated Encryption: AES-256-GCM with unique 12-byte IVs per credential entry.
  • Key Derivation: PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 with 600,000 iterations for master keys and 100,000 for local PIN envelopes.
  • Local Vault Storage: Persisted in browser IndexedDB with an integrity check canary. Zero external databases.
  • User-Owned Sync: Optional direct client-side backup to your personal Google Drive (appDataFolder) via OAuth 2.0 PKCE.
  • Local Agent Bridge (MCP): Model Context Protocol bridge on 127.0.0.1 for local developer tools (Claude Desktop, Cursor).
  • Client-Side Vault Migration: One-click import from Bitwarden, 1Password, Chrome, Apple Passwords, and LastPass.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where are my passwords stored? Exclusively in your local device's browser IndexedDB storage unless you explicitly choose to backup encrypted ciphertext to your personal Google Drive.

Can the creator or Google read my passwords? No. Decryption occurs solely inside window.crypto.subtle in your browser tab using keys derived from your 12-word paper phrase.
